1. System Overview
The game is an AR-supported Trading Card Game in which physical cards are recognized via barcode scanning and managed digitally.
Interactions are performed via voice commands.


2. Card Structure
2.1 Numbering
Player Cards: 1 to 10
Enemy Cards: -1 to -10

This number also serves as a unique identifier within the system.

2.2 Card States
Inactive: Not yet scanned
Active: Scanned and in play
Destroyed: HP ≤ 0, no longer active in the game

2.3 Activation
Cards are recognized via barcode scanning
After a successful scan:
The card is added to the game
State changes to active
An active card remains in the game until its HP reaches 0


3. Input System
3.1 Voice Command
Attacks are triggered exclusively via the following command:

"Ich spiele die Karte X"

X corresponds to the card number
The command must be clearly assignable to an active card


4. Game Flow
4.1 Turn Structure
The game is turn-based
Player and enemy perform actions alternately
Each turn consists of exactly one attack

4.2 Attack Resolution
The attack is initiated via the voice command
The selected card performs the action
An attack always affects all enemy cards simultaneously


5. Damage System
5.1 Card Damage
Each attacking card deals damage to all enemy cards
Damage directly reduces the HP of affected cards

5.2 Player Damage
Player damage depends on the number of active cards:

Number of active cards
3 or more      Card damage only
2 or fewer     Card damage + player damage


6. Effect System
6.1 General Rules
Effects are applied by cards
A card can only have exactly one effect at a time
If a new effect is applied:
The existing effect is completely removed

6.2 Effect Types

Fire
Deals periodic damage (tick damage)
Stacking mechanic:
Stacks increase in steps of 2
No upper limit on stacks

Frost
Also deals periodic damage
Identical behavior to Fire:
Stacks increase in steps of 2
Unlimited maximum stacks

Cards can receive additional damage from Fire or Frost if they have a weakness

Poison
Reduces the damage dealt by the affected card
Effect is based on a percentage value
Maximum reduction is limited (defined per card)


7. Area Effects
Each attack acts as an area-of-effect (AoE)
There is no target selection
All enemy cards are affected simultaneously


8. State Changes
8.1 Cards
HP is reduced by damage
If HP ≤ 0:
Card is marked as destroyed
Card no longer participates in the game

8.2 Player
Player has their own HP
Player damage is only applied under defined conditions


9. Win and Loss Condition
A player loses when their player HP reaches 0 or below
Card status has no direct impact on defeat except through the player damage rule
